# DESIGN.md — Koneksa Indonesia

## Brand Identity & Direction

- **Brand Name**: Koneksa Indonesia
- **Tagline**: "Solusi Digital, Mitra Terpercaya."
- **Origin**: Surabaya, Jawa Timur (Jl. Sidosermo Gg. Damri No.39, Wonocolo)
- **Target Audience**: UMKM, Profesional, dan Korporasi di seluruh Indonesia yang membutuhkan digitalisasi tepat guna, efisien, dan andal.

## Visual Tone & Aesthetic

- **Design Dial**: ENERGY 2 (Balanced & Trustworthy) / RHYTHM 2 (Structured with Editorial Accent) / MOTION 2 (Smooth Reveal & Tactile Hover)
- **Palette**:
  - Primary Brand: Deep Navy (`#0B1B33`) taken directly from the Koneksa logo
  - Secondary Accent: Royal Sapphire (`#1D4ED8`) & Active Azure (`#2563EB`)
  - Background Neutral: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) & Subtle Cool Slate (`#F8FAFC` / `#F1F5F9`)
  - Text: Slate 900 (`#0F172A`) for high-contrast headlines (passes WCAG AAA > 12:1), Slate 600 (`#475569`) for readable body text
  - Dark Mode Accent: Midnight Navy (`#060D1A`), Surface Navy (`#0D192E`), Border Slate (`#1E293B`)
- **Typography**:
  - Primary Sans: Geist Sans (`font-sans`), clean modern geometric sans-serif
  - Headline hierarchy: High weight contrast, generous line-height, no oversized monospace gimmicks
- **Anti-Slop Guarantees**:
  - No random em-dashes (`—`) in copy
  - No fake statistics or fabricated testimonials
  - Real client names (SMA N 14, ATM Studio, PLN Icon Plus)
  - Genuine pricing packages with explicit deliverables
  - Real interactive WhatsApp links with pre-filled greeting messages
  - Genuine addresses and direct contact information
